Ten Scholars of Opportunity عُلَمَاءُ الفُرَص

Ten trusted voices of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama‘ah, from the Tabi‘un to the later centuries, whose teaching or lived example turned time, knowledge and livelihood into benefit for ordinary believers.
Authenticity note: each entry is tagged — Quoted (wording widely reported), Attributed (verse or saying ascribed to the scholar), Teaching summary (my paraphrase of the book's theme) or Lived example. Before teaching, publishing or selling this content, verify wording against the cited source with a qualified teacher. This app is educational and is not a fatwa.

Report & Data التقرير

Your data is stored only in this browser (local storage). Use “Save my data” to keep a backup file. “Clear ALL” erases every status, note and log entry.

How to Use This App

  1. Explore the Tree. Start at the roots (Niyyah, ‘Ilm, Tawhid, Tawakkul), then climb any Deen or Dunya branch. Each box has an Arabic term, its English parallel, a definition, a concrete behavior and a guiding scholar.
  2. Set a status. Mark each level Planned, Practicing or Mastered, and add a personal note and target date. Pulsing boxes show your next step.
  3. Keep the log. Under My Journey, record real opportunities and your response.
  4. Read the Ten Scholars for the sayings and examples behind the branches.
  5. Report. Download a PDF, print it, or back up / restore your data. Remove the sample data first, then clear or edit anything at any time.
Creed-to-Commerce™ principle: every business and life activity flows from, and stays accountable to, a creed. This tool helps the believer convert each fursah into worship and benefit, without neglecting a rightful share of this world (Qur’an 28:77).
